# Data-access request to the TT Elite Series organiser

Send this from your own email to the contact address on eliteseries.eu (look for "Kontakt"). Written permission or an
export is the only free source that is both reliable and clearly allowed.

---

**Subject:** Request: permission to use TT Elite Series results for personal statistical research / Prośba o zgodę na wykorzystanie wyników

**English**

Hello,

I am an individual table tennis fan in the United States building a personal, non-commercial statistical model of
TT Elite Series matches (player ratings and match-outcome probabilities).

Could you tell me whether either of these is acceptable?
1. Reading your public results and schedule pages with an automated script, at a low rate (one request every few
   minutes, identified user agent, no load on your servers), for personal use only; or
2. Receiving a data export (CSV or similar) of historical results with set scores.

I will not republish or resell the data, and I am happy to follow any conditions you set.

Thank you,
[Your name]
